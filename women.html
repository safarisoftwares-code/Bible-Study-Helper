📖 This is a study book, not the Bible. This page is a human-written commentary on women in Scripture. It is not a replacement for the Bible, and it is not exhaustive — many women, passages, and themes in Scripture are not covered here. Always read the Bible itself. What this book is — and isn't

  • It is a devotional celebration of women of faith, courage, service, prayer and prophecy in Scripture.
  • It is not the Bible. Scripture is the authority; this book is a fallible human aid to reading it.
  • It is not exhaustive. Scripture records well over a hundred women. This book highlights a selection. Many are not mentioned here.
  • It is not a position paper. It does not address contested questions about roles in public worship. Those deserve their own careful, prayerful study — in the Bible, with your congregation.
  • It quotes by reference, not by replacement. Every verse cited links to the full text in the Safari Reference Bible.

Read the Bible first. Read this second. Where they differ, the Bible wins.

Women of Faith — A Devotional Catalog

1. Prophetesses

MiriamExodus 15:20 ↗
She led Israel in song after the sea split, and her voice became the first recorded worship chorus in Scripture.
DeborahJudges 4:4 ↗
Prophetess and judge, she spoke God's word to a nation and a general listened.
Huldah2 Kings 22:14 ↗
When the lost Law was found, the king sent for her — and she spoke the word that launched a national reform.
AnnaLuke 2:36 ↗
Eighty-four years of widowhood, fasting and prayer — and she was in the temple the day the Messiah arrived.
Philip's four daughtersActs 21:9 ↗
Unnamed, unmarried, and prophesying — a quiet sign that the Spirit had been poured out on daughters as well as sons.

2. Leaders & Judges

DeborahJudges 4–5 ↗
"Mother of Israel" — she held court, summoned a general, and saw God rout an army.
EstherEsther 4:16 ↗
"If I perish, I perish" — a queen who risked her life to save a people who could not save themselves.
JaelJudges 4:21 ↗
The tent-peg and the hammer — a woman who ended a war with the tools at hand.
Zelophehad's daughtersNumbers 27:1–11 ↗
Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah — five sisters who brought a case to Moses and changed Israel's inheritance law.
Daughters of ShallumNehemiah 3:12 ↗
They rebuilt the wall alongside the men — their names in the record, their work in the stones.

3. Women Who Saved Lives

Shiphrah & PuahExodus 1:17 ↗
Two midwives who feared God more than Pharaoh, and refused to kill the babies.
JochebedExodus 2:2 ↗
A mother's basket, a river, a princess — and the deliverer of Israel was preserved.
Pharaoh's daughterExodus 2:5–10 ↗
A princess's compassion turned a death sentence into a rescue.
RahabJoshua 2 ↗
A woman of the city who hid spies, believed in a God she had only heard of, and was grafted into the line of Christ.
Abigail1 Samuel 25 ↗
A wise woman who stopped a war between two men with a well-timed word.
Wise Woman of Abel2 Samuel 20 ↗
She negotiated peace for her city while the army was already at the gate.
Jehosheba2 Kings 11:2 ↗
A princess who hid a baby in the temple and preserved the line of David.
EstherEsther 4:16 ↗
She risked death to stand between a people and their executioner.
Pilate's wifeMatthew 27:19 ↗
"Have nothing to do with that righteous man" — a warning from a dream, spoken to a governor who did not listen.

4. Women of Exceptional Faith

SarahHebrews 11:11 ↗
She laughed at the promise, then received the son — faith that grew through doubt.
RahabHebrews 11:31 ↗
Faith that put her whole household in a window, and her name in the genealogy of Jesus.
Hannah1 Samuel 1 ↗
She prayed through years of barrenness and grief, and God answered with a prophet.
RuthRuth 1:16 ↗
"Your God will be my God" — a foreigner who bound herself to an old woman and a covenant she had no right to.
The Shunammite woman2 Kings 4:8 ↗
She built a room for a prophet she did not yet understand, and received her son back from the dead.
The Canaanite womanMatthew 15:28 ↗
She would not be turned away — a persistent faith that Jesus commended as great.
The woman with the issue of bloodMark 5:34 ↗
Twelve years bleeding, a touch in a crowd, and a "Daughter, your faith has healed you."
MaryLuke 1:38 ↗
"Behold, I am the servant of the Lord" — the yes that opened the door of the incarnation.
AnnaLuke 2:37 ↗
She stayed in the temple, praying, until the Messiah arrived.
Lois & Eunice2 Timothy 1:5 ↗
A grandmother and mother whose sincere faith was transmitted to Timothy across two generations.

5. Women Who Served & Supported Ministry

The women who followed JesusLuke 8:1–3 ↗
Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Susanna and others — they funded the ministry out of their own means.
Martha & Mary of BethanyJohn 11–12 ↗
One served at table, one sat at His feet — both loved the same Lord in the same house.
LydiaActs 16:15 ↗
A businesswoman who opened her home and her purse to the first church on European soil.
PriscillaActs 18 ↗
A co-worker with Paul, a teacher of Apollos, and a host of a church in her house.
PhoebeRomans 16:1 ↗
A diakonos of the church at Cenchreae, entrusted with Paul's letter to the Romans.
Dorcas (Tabitha)Acts 9:36 ↗
Full of good works and charity; when she died, the whole city of Joppa wept and Peter was sent for.
The widow with two mitesMark 12:41–44 ↗
She gave more than the wealthy, because she gave out of her poverty.

6. Women Who Taught & Discipled

PriscillaActs 18:26 ↗
She and Aquila took Apollos aside and explained the way of God more accurately — a teacher in the church.
Lois & Eunice2 Timothy 1:5 ↗
They taught Timothy the Scriptures from childhood, and Paul honored them by name.
Older womenTitus 2:3–5 ↗
Teachers of good, models of reverence, mentors of younger women — a structural role in the church's discipleship.
The Proverbs 31 womanProverbs 31:10–31 ↗
A poem of a woman who fears the Lord — industrious, wise, generous, and to be praised.

7. Women Who Evangelized

The Samaritan womanJohn 4:29 ↗
"Come, see a man who told me everything I ever did" — the first evangelist in John's Gospel.
Mary MagdaleneJohn 20:18 ↗
The first witness of the resurrection, sent to tell the disciples — "the apostle to the apostles."
The women at the tombMatthew 28:1–10 ↗
They came to anoint a body and left with a commission.
Priscilla & AquilaActs 18 ↗
They took Apollos home and taught him — evangelism and discipleship in the same room.

8. Women Who Prayed

Hannah1 Samuel 1 ↗
Her prayer in the temple changed a nation — and her song became a template for Mary's.
AnnaLuke 2:37 ↗
A widow who "did not depart from the temple" — prayer as a life-shape.
The women in the upper roomActs 1:14 ↗
Mary, and the women, and the disciples — together in prayer when the Spirit fell.
Euodia & SyntychePhilippians 4:2–3 ↗
Two women who labored in the gospel, urged by Paul to agree in the Lord.

9. Women Who Hosted Churches

LydiaActs 16:15 ↗
She opened her home and the church in Philippi began there.
PriscillaRomans 16:3–5 ↗
"The church in their house" — a household gathered around the gospel.
NymphaColossians 4:15 ↗
"The church in her house" — a woman whose home was a congregation.
ApphiaPhilemon 2 ↗
Greeted by Paul alongside Philemon and Archippus — likely the host of the church in Colossae.

10. Women of Courage & Justice

DeborahJudges 4–5 ↗
She judged, she summoned, she sang — courage in every register.
JaelJudges 4:21 ↗
She acted when the men hesitated.
EstherEsther 4:16 ↗
She fasted, she dressed, she went in — courage in the face of a king's law.
VashtiEsther 1:12 ↗
She refused to be displayed — a "no" that protected her dignity and prepared the way for Esther's "yes."
Rizpah2 Samuel 21:10 ↗
She guarded her sons' bodies for months — a mother's persistent love that moved a king.
Jehosheba2 Kings 11:2 ↗
She hid a child in a temple and saved a dynasty.
Shiphrah & PuahExodus 1:17 ↗
Two women who refused an empire's order.
Tamar (Judah's daughter-in-law)Genesis 38:26 ↗
She pursued justice at great cost — and Judah said, "She is more righteous than I."
Zelophehad's daughtersNumbers 27:1–11 ↗
They brought a case to Moses and changed the law.
Wise Woman of Tekoa2 Samuel 14 ↗
She came to David with a story and left with a reconciliation.
Wise Woman of Abel2 Samuel 20 ↗
She saved her city by her words.
